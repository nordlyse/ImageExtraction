About

ImageExtraction opens two photos side by side. SAM splits each photo into pieces. Copy a piece from one photo and paste it onto the other. Then enlarge, shrink, or delete that piece.

Help

Press Open on a pane, or drop a JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo onto that pane. When the split finishes, move over a piece to see its outline. Right-click that piece and choose Copy or Delete. On the other photo, click the place you want, then right-click and choose Paste. The piece is placed with its center on that point. Right-click the pasted piece and choose Enlarge or Shrink. Each step changes the size by a quarter, and the piece stays centered.
